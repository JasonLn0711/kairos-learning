# M02-02 Reference answers

## answer-roots | 1. Three URLs are not three independent origins

No. All three trace back to one forum post. Seek original records obtained through different routes, matching the same time, subject, and claim. A fourth repost is not independent corroboration.

## answer-time | 2. Do not mix dates and environments

No. Downloading an old document today does not update its contents. A desired skill is not a deployment record, and testing is not production. Even confirmed use of X requires separately authorized checks of version, configuration, and vulnerability applicability before a weakness can be established.

## answer-hash | 3. A hash compares content; it does not endorse a story

With a reliable baseline, a matching digest strongly supports that preserved content has not changed. It does not establish that the screenshot was originally authentic, who created it, its capture time, or whether important context was omitted. It is not an absolute mathematical collision guarantee. A fabricated file can also have a correctly calculated hash.

## answer-missing | 4. Search gaps are not proof of asset absence

Do not delete it solely on that basis. Record that it was not found within the specified sources, queries, and time, retain coverage limitations, and verify through authorized current asset records or the responsible owner. Do not probe an out-of-scope target merely to fill the gap.
