# M09-04 | Visual reasoning reference answers

> Initially collapsed on the website. These are example explanations. Reading them is not the same as completing the understanding check.

## 1. Which boundary cannot inherit the employee's authorization?

The visitor's path from the public area into the controlled area does not automatically become valid when the employee's badge succeeds. The employee's credential and rights belong to that employee. An open door is a device state, not proof of the visitor's identity, work approval, or permitted scope. A visitor might already have a legitimate arrangement, so check the process rather than judging from appearance.

## 2. Concepts and evidence

An invented urgent-maintenance justification may be pretexting. Free tools used to induce use of an unknown device may be baiting. Entry behind an authorized person without required authorization may be tailgating. They may coexist. However, missing work and visitor-approval evidence means there are risks to investigate, not enough evidence to declare every action malicious, code execution established, or data exfiltrated.

## 3. What to stop and what to verify

Do not use the unknown USB drive or provide additional information or access. Follow the established reception, responsible-contact, security, or IT process to verify work, identity, and scope. Prioritize personal safety; do not block or chase the person yourself. Preserve existing times, locations, and statements. Where authorized and necessary, correlate visitor records, access events and relevant video, device connections, and execution records. Those records may be absent or incomplete. Mark uncertainty instead of treating missing records as proof of no event or creating a new hazardous test.

**Reasoning goal: identify the crossed boundary, seek evidence that can verify it, and act on the known risk. Guessing more attack labels is not a substitute.**
