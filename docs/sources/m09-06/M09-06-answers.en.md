# M09-06 | Reference answers

## 1. Why is the new number insufficiently independent?
The request and telephone number may be controlled by the same party. Reconfirmation does not establish a trusted connection to the particular request. Use previously verified records or the established system to verify data, destination, purpose, and approval. Switching apps or asking twice is insufficient; trusted channels also need maintenance.

## 2. Why do DMARC and phishing-resistant sign-in not authorize the upload?
DMARC checks domain authentication and alignment; phishing-resistant authentication protects sign-in. Neither approves the purpose or destination of a data transfer. Independently verify the request, sharing authority, approved recipients, minimum data, and required review. A genuine account can be abused, and a genuine user can be manipulated.

## 3. What is known and what should happen after password entry?
Password entry is known and authentication material may be exposed. Successful takeover or data disclosure is not yet established. Use a trusted entry point to follow the password-change process, report, and preserve the message and time. Appropriate administrators should review sign-ins, recovery settings, and active sessions and revoke access as needed. Do not wait for visible damage or assume a password change handled every session.
